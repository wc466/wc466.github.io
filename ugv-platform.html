Autonomous off-road platform

Most of this work ran on a modified John Deere R-Gator, a 6×4 unmanned utility vehicle with strong off-road capability. It started as a bare-bones platform with no localization or perception, and I helped turn it into a research vehicle: sensor mount, calibrated LiDAR and stereo, EKF odometry, and ROS2 integration. I also worked across a fleet of Clearpath Robotics platforms (Husky, Jackal, Warthog).

Results in progressThe platform is operational and was used for every dataset on this site. A full integration write-up (architecture, networking, ROS2 graph) is coming.

R-Gator & Warthog

Large platforms for unmanned transport and construction tasks in rough terrain. They carry the OS1-128 + MultiSense S27 perception head.

Husky & Jackal

Smaller Clearpath robots for surveillance and prototyping. New perception and mapping stacks were tried here before going on the big vehicles.

Software

ROS1 (Melodic, Noetic) and ROS2 (Humble), with Docker to run ROS1-only packages next to ROS2 nodes. See sensing stack and terrain mapping.
